Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions after your finance application was declined? We have answers.

How long does the flow take?

Depending on the barrier, removing a default or judgment can take anywhere from 21 to 45 business days once the dispute is formally lodged.

Can I go back to the same dealership?

Yes! Once we provide you with your updated credit clearance, you can take it straight back to the Finance and Insurance (F&I) manager at your dealership to re-apply.

Do you guarantee I will get the car?

We guarantee the legal removal of inaccurate or paid-up barriers. While we cannot guarantee bank approval (as that depends on your current salary), removing these barriers drastically increases your chances.
